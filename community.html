Our Commitment

Lubango Arts is deeply committed to giving back to the community. We believe in creating opportunities for minorities including women, disabled people, and young people. Our work revolves around a strong ethic of respect for all, irrespective of sex, race, religion, or social identification.

Through our community initiatives, we aim to use the power of storytelling and media to raise awareness, educate, and inspire positive change in Zambian society and beyond.

Our Initiatives

Community School Support

Education is the foundation of opportunity. Lubango Arts supports community schools in underserved areas of Zambia, providing resources, mentorship, and creative arts programs that help children discover their potential.

Our programs focus on creative expression through film, storytelling, and visual arts, giving students skills that prepare them for the modern world while honoring their cultural heritage.

  • Educational resources and materials
  • Creative arts workshops
  • Mentorship programs
  • Technology access initiatives

Human Trafficking Awareness

In partnership with Talitha Kum Zambia (Takuza), we have produced powerful short films that raise awareness about the realities of human trafficking. These films serve as educational tools and advocacy resources used across Zambia and the region.

Films like "Devil in the Details" and "Open Your Eyes" have helped bring this critical issue to public attention, sparking conversations and inspiring action in communities.

  • Documentary filmmaking for awareness
  • Community screening events
  • Educational partnerships
  • Survivor support advocacy

Health Awareness Campaigns

We collaborate with organizations like the Zambia Cancer Society to create media content that educates communities about health issues. Through compelling storytelling, we help make complex health information accessible and actionable.

Our media campaigns have reached thousands of Zambians, encouraging early screening, healthy lifestyle choices, and community support for those affected by illness.

  • Cancer awareness content
  • Public health messaging
  • Community health events
  • Prevention education

Youth Empowerment

Young people are the future of Zambian storytelling. We provide training and mentorship opportunities for aspiring filmmakers, content creators, and media professionals from underserved backgrounds.

Through hands-on workshops and real production experience, we help young Zambians develop skills that can transform their lives and their communities.

  • Filmmaking training programs
  • Internship opportunities
  • Equipment access
  • Career mentorship
