We craft stories that matter

Lubango Arts is a film and television production company based in Lusaka, Zambia. We create compelling content that resonates with African audiences and beyond.

What We Do

Film Production

We produce feature films, documentaries, and short films that tell authentic African stories with professional excellence and cinematic quality.

Television Series

From reality shows to scripted series, we create engaging television content for leading networks including Zambezi Magic and Showmax.

Digital Content

We develop digital-first content, social media campaigns, and online video series tailored for modern platforms and audiences.

Voice & Audio

Professional voice-over services, radio dramas, and audio narration in English, Tonga, Bemba, and Nyanja.

Our Approach

We believe in the power of storytelling to connect, inspire, and create change. Our production philosophy combines technical excellence with cultural authenticity, ensuring every project resonates with its intended audience while maintaining the highest production standards.

From concept development through post-production, we handle every aspect of the filmmaking process with meticulous attention to detail and creative vision.

Our Impact

Lubango Arts has pioneered several firsts in Zambian television, including producing the country's first original reality dating show, Wanilata. We continue to push boundaries and create opportunities for local talent while contributing to the growth of Zambia's film and television industry.
