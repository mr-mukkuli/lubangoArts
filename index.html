About Lubango Arts

We craft stories that matter. Lubango Arts is a film and television production company based in Lusaka, Zambia, creating compelling content that resonates with African audiences and beyond.

What We Do

We produce feature films, documentaries, television series, and digital content that tell authentic African stories with professional excellence and cinematic quality.

Our Approach

We believe in the power of storytelling to connect, inspire, and create change. Our production philosophy combines technical excellence with cultural authenticity.
