About Lubango Arts

Lubango Arts is a Communications Consultancy Firm registered in Zambia, specializing in content creation, curation, and management.

What We Do

We specialize in Film, Reality TV, Documentaries, Photography, Live Streaming, and Digital Content. Our services extend to Actor Coaching, Creative Directing, Crew Supply, and comprehensive Filmmaking Consultancy from research through post-production quality control.

Our Expertise

Beyond production, we excel in Media Mobilization, Social Media Management, Content Curation, Branding, Translation, Copy Editing, Publicity, and Events Planning. We also train organizations on social media and online tools to increase their digital presence.

Our Reach

Our work extends beyond Zambia to Senegal, Kenya, Ethiopia (Addis Ababa), and Zimbabwe. We serve a diverse clientele including NGOs, Media Organizations, Corporates, International Civil Society Organizations, Celebrities, and Small-scale Businesses.

Community Impact

Lubango Arts strives to create opportunities for minorities including women, disabled people, and young people. Our work revolves around a strong ethic of respect for all, irrespective of sex, race, religion, or social identification. We are actively involved in charity causes.
